# Inspiration Point to Vincent Gap

Nov 29, 2025 — by [Tilman](https://pct.ahoh.de/author/tilman/) in [Southern California](https://pct.ahoh.de/category/southern-california/)

**Start:** [34.372542, -117.710506](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KjGHfpqma2muRU3DA) (Inspiration Point Trailhead)  
**End:** [34.372542, -117.710506](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KjGHfpqma2muRU3DA) (Inspiration Point Trailhead)

**Distance:** 10.6 mi (up to PCT mile 374.0, 14.1% completed)  
**Time:** 4 hours 3 minutes (start 9:56am, end 2:00pm)  
**Elevation Gain:** 2,695 ft (low 6,582 ft – high 7,504 ft)  
**Elevation Loss:** 2,695 ft (start 7,364 ft – end 7,364 ft)

---

The day was not off to a good start. I was ready to leave at 7:10am. But while loading the car, it locked itself with the car key inside. I was furious. This had never happened to me before. The first locksmith in the area opened at 8am, but didn’t have anyone available to come. They texted me a few other phone numbers but those were all unavailable until 9am. At 9am sharp, I was able to get a hold of another locksmith who arrived 15 minutes later. Within little more than a minute, they had pried open the passenger door and opened it from the inside. I was shocked how easy it was with the right tools. A few minutes later (and $110 poorer) I was on my way to Inspiration Point.

I had only planned for an out-and-back hike from Inspiration Point to Vincent Gap and there was still enough time in the day to do that and drive home. From the trailhead, the PCT went uphill to a rocky outcrop overlooking the San Gabriel River drainage with the familiar snow covered mountains to the east and west. The trail had plenty of patchy snow, a bit more on the northern slopes, but never more than a foot and always with well defined boot tracks which made it fairly easy to follow. The first mile was still very visibly impacted by the 2024 Bridge Fire with plenty of charred trees and even a collapsed radio tower which had seen it’s metal structure melted. As the trail moved past the Grassy Hollow Picnic Site, it entered unharmed pine forest on the northern side of Blue Ridge. Views of the desert to the north soon opened up. The contrast between the snowy ground and the desert in the distance was mind blowing.

The trail briefly followed paved Forest Service Road 3N26.1 before retreating to the forested northern slope again. Gentle ups and downs made it easy to hike. I passed a side trail to Jackson Lake. Soon after, the trail turned south and started its steep descent towards Vincent Gap. The vegetation changed very quickly from pine forest to oak trees and it was much warmer all of the sudden. Views were few, but I could see the Prairie Form valley I had taken up to Blue Ridge on Sunday. I reached Vincent Gap and had lunch on a bench with great views of Mount Baldy, then retraced my steps back to Inspiration Point back up the steep slope of Blue Ridge.

---

**Trail Conditions:** Patchy snow throughout most of the hike, but with easy-to-follow boot tracks. Significant impact from the 2024 Bridge Fire for the first mile.

**Parking:** There is plenty of parking on Inspiration Point.

**Crowds:** Fairly solitary. Only met 14 people throughout the day.

**Weather:** Mostly sunny with blue skies with a few cirrus clouds. Low of 50°F / 10°C on the northern slope. High of 78°F / 26°C in the sun while taking a break at Vincent Gap.

**Land Manager & Permits:** Angeles National Forest. San Gabriel Mountains National Monument. Annual Interagency Permit required.

**Overall Rating:** 3.8

Difficulty: Moderate  
Scenery: Great  
Logistics: Very easy
